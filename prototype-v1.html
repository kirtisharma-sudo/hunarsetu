        Problem Brief
        
      

      
Skilled hands, no paper trail

      

        
Problem
About 80% of informal blue-collar workers have no verifiable credentials or text resume, so skilled electricians and technicians lose out on steady work and fair wages.

        
Why existing tools fail
White-collar portals demand English text resumes. Generic video apps have no trusted verification layer.

        
HunarSetu
Voice bio in the worker's language, a 15-second task video screened by a multi-agent Vision AI, and a voice vouch from a Master Technician. The result is a QR skill card a contractor can trust and act on via WhatsApp.
