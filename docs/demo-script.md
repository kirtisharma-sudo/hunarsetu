# Demo Script

About 3 minutes. For a 60-second version, skip steps 6, 8 and 10.

**Setup.** Open the deployed HTTPS URL, or `index.html`, in Chrome at a phone-sized or desktop window. Allow the microphone. Open the Demo guide (sliders button, top right) and choose **Reset demo** for a clean start. The guide lists the same nine journey steps and jumps to the right role.

1. **Problem (10 s).** A skilled electrician has no resume, so he cannot prove his skill or his work history.
2. **Worker: voice profile.** On Home, tap the microphone, speak your name, years of work and skills, then stop and play it back. If the browser cannot transcribe, nothing is invented: use *Use sample text (demo)*, which is labelled Sample. Check the filled details and save.
3. **Worker: skill test.** My Hunar Card, *Take a skill test*, pick *Distribution board assembly*, upload a short video (`tests/sample.mp4` works). Point out that the basic checks are hints, not a safety certificate.
4. **Ustad: review.** Switch role to Ustad, Pending Assessments, open Ramesh's video, add a voice note or comment, then *Approve skill*.
5. **Worker: Hunar Card.** Switch back to Worker, My Hunar Card. Show the endorsement and QR. Tap Open to show the public page, which has no phone number.
6. **Contractor: post a job.** Post a Job, for example Dwarka, fixed Rs. 4,500.
7. **Apply and hire.** Worker: Find Work, Apply. Contractor: Applications, Accept, Assign job.
8. **Work and confirmation.** Worker: Kaam Ka Khata, add a note and photo, send. Contractor: Work History, rate and confirm. Worker confirms. The job now shows as verified.
9. **Payment.** Contractor: Payments, add an advance of Rs. 1,500. Worker confirms. Rs. 3,000 shows as outstanding.
10. **Hunar Mitra (optional).** Assist Registration: show that the consent checkbox is required.
11. **Close (10 s).** Skills, trust, work history, reputation. State that this is a prototype with browser-local data.

**Say plainly during the demo:** role switching is not a login; sample records are marked Sample; the ledger records agreements and does not guarantee payment; the QR works from another phone only when the app is hosted at an HTTPS address.
